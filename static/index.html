Credit Risk Ledger

A model that scores a loan application's probability of default, then weighs that probability against a threshold tuned for precision and recall rather than accuracy alone. The output isn't just a score — it's calibrated, so a reading of 0.70 means roughly seven defaults in ten, not merely a higher number than the applicant before it.
